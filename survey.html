    How to survey a field
    • Record point: stand on the spot (a fence corner, the end of the pitch, a tree trunk), keep still and let it average the GPS. Longer is more accurate; 15 s is a good default.
    • Photo here: a quick point at your current position with a photo and the compass direction you faced. Good for showing what things look like (fence type, hedge height).
    • Walk a line: walk along a fence, hedge or the edge of the mown grass; it adds a point every 3 m. Tap Finish at the end.
    • Use 📷 on any item to add more photos, ✎ to add a note such as "about 2 m high".
    • GPS is best in the open, worse next to trees and buildings: aim for ±5 m or better (green).
    • Everything stays on this phone until you clear it. Export zip to share it (Drive, email…) and hand it over for the sim.
